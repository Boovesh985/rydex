# Code of Conduct

Rydex follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and experience level, and focus on what is best for the project. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in the repository or in any space connected to it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@Boovesh985](https://github.com/Boovesh985), privately (through the contact details on their GitHub profile). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, code or issues, or ban contributors, temporarily or permanently, for behaviour that breaks this code.
